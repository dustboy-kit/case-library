PM2.5 / OpenSCAD design studies

One monitor.
100 enclosures.

Ten body shapes × ten grille patterns. Each STL contains a separate lid and base, placed flat beside each other. All versions share the offset OLED window and a nominal USB opening. Dimensions are estimated from photos; fit, fastening and airflow have not been tested.
